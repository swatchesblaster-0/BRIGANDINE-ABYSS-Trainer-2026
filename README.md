## 🎮 About the Game

BRIGANDINE ABYSS is a turn-based tactical strategy RPG and war simulation game developed by Adglobe and published by NIS America and Happinet. It launched on Steam on August 26, 2026, with console versions following on August 27, 2026. Players command one of six factions in Story Mode or choose from 24 nations in Mission Mode while fighting for control of Meltitea.

This trainer provides optional quality-of-life enhancements for BRIGANDINE ABYSS, supporting nation management, troop development, equipment use, exploration, and turn-based hex-grid battles. ## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

BRIGANDINE ABYSS separates strategic preparation from combat. Use the Organization Phase to position leaders, arrange monster squads, assign equipment, and prepare attacks. During battles, units operate on a hexagonal grid. Activate movement-related options only after an allied unit is selected, and turn them off before ending the unit's action when possible.

### Tools & Inventory

Use the trainer's resource and equipment options while managing the army between battles. Equipment supports troop preparation, while resources are used for recruitment, stronghold development, and other nation-management tasks. Save before purchasing, assigning, or changing equipment so the original state can be restored safely.

### World & Environment

Story Mode follows six faction campaigns connected to the return of the Abyssloa Empire. Mission Mode offers 24 playable nations with individual victory conditions. Develop strongholds, recruit leaders and monsters, send available leaders on explorations, and choose when to invade neighboring nations for resources and military advantages.

### Combat & Weapons

Battles are turn-based and take place on hexagonal maps. Leaders command squads of monsters, and defeating an opposing leader can force that squad to retreat or end its participation in the battle. Use health, damage, movement, and experience options only for allied units unless you intentionally want to alter the encounter balance.
